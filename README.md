# Enhanced War Client

A mod for HELLDIVERS (2015), for [Obsidian](https://github.com/Alomare/ObsidianLauncher): the client side of Enhanced wars in Obsidian Commander.

Enhanced wars build on the Vanilla Galactic War of Commander, with the same fronts and rules. Custom events, order support and subfactions come later. Every player in an Enhanced war needs this mod. Obsidian installs and enables it when an Enhanced war is created, selected or joined.

## What it does

For now, it detects an Enhanced war and notes it in the loader log. Outside an Enhanced war (the official war or a Vanilla war), it changes nothing.

## Playing with others

A client mod. Every player in an Enhanced war needs it, and joining any other lobby works as without the mod.

Needs Obsidian 2.0.0 or later, which tells mods which war is being played.
