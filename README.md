# Enhanced War Client

A mod for HELLDIVERS (2015), for [Obsidian](https://github.com/Alomare/ObsidianLauncher): the client side of Enhanced wars in Obsidian Commander.

An Enhanced war builds on the Vanilla Galactic War of Commander, with the same fronts and rules, and adds custom events, order support and subfactions later on. Every player in an Enhanced war runs this mod. Obsidian installs and enables it when an Enhanced war is created, selected or joined.

## What it does

- Detects an Enhanced war and notes it in the loader log.
- Nothing else yet. Outside an Enhanced war (the official war, a Vanilla war) it changes nothing.

## Playing with others

A client mod. Every player of an Enhanced war needs it; joining any other lobby works the same as without the mod.

Needs Obsidian 2.0.0 or later, which tells mods which war is played.
